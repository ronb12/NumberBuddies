# Number Buddies Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 19, 2026  
**Developer:** Bradley Virtual Solutions, LLC

Number Buddies is a kids math practice app for ages 6–8. We designed it so families can use the app without creating accounts or sharing personal information.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.**

All practice progress, stars, levels, and profile names stay on your device.

## Third-party services

Number Buddies does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, or social sign-in.

The optional read-aloud feature uses Apple’s on-device speech synthesizer.

## Children’s privacy

Number Buddies is intended for children and parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no data leaves the device, there is no account registration and no online profile.
